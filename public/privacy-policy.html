Effective date: August 27, 2026

AI House Plan Matchmaker, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates PlanMatch (the “Service”), available at planmatch.dev. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Home description text. When you describe your dream home, that text is processed to generate your matches. We do not store this text after your session ends.
  • Selections you make. Style and feature preferences you select via buttons or chips.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and device type, collected automatically by our hosting provider’s server logs.

We do not currently require account creation, and we do not collect payment information — purchases happen on third-party sites you’re referred to (see Section 3).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to: - Generate your house plan matches and the explanations shown alongside them - Improve the accuracy and relevance of our matching - Monitor and improve the performance and reliability of the Service - Diagnose technical problems

3. Third-Party Services

To provide the Service, we share limited data with:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI). The home description you enter is sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to extract structured search criteria and generate match explanations. This text is transmitted to Anthropic for processing. See Anthropic’s Privacy Policy for how they handle this data.
  • Third-party house plan providers. When you click a plan link, you leave our Service and go to one of these third-party sites. Some of these links may become affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you make a purchase (see our FTC Affiliate Disclosure). We do not control, and are not responsible for, the privacy practices of these third-party sites — review their own privacy policies before providing any information to them.
  • Vercel, our hosting provider, which processes technical and usage data (such as request logs) on our behalf. We do not currently use a separate analytics provider.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We do not retain your home description text beyond the session needed to generate your matches.

Aggregated, de-identified usage analytics may be retained longer for product improvement purposes.
